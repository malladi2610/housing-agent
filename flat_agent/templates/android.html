The running pipeline

  1. Refresh bounded feed pages and deduplicate listing identities.
  2. Read ad details into the persistent tracker.
  3. Extract with GLM; validate costs, dates and commute facts.
  4. Draft with Kimi using your saved introduction.
  5. Reopen and compare the live listing before sending.
  6. Verify the conversation and exact outgoing message; save delivery evidence.

Automatic initial enquiries and optionally one follow-up use Europe/Berlin contact hours. Replies can be drafted for approval; viewings remain proposed until accepted by the advertiser. Missing or changed facts stop a send.
